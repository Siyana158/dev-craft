Before you order

How fresh is it?

We roast on Tuesdays and post on Wednesdays. Most orders reach Chennai the next day and the rest of India within seven days.

What if I don't like it?

Write to us within 14 days and we will send a different tin or refund you. You do not need to send the tea back.

Can I buy a single cup?

Not yet. The smallest tin is 50 g, which makes about sixteen cups.

Do you ship abroad?

Not at the moment. India only, with tracked courier.
