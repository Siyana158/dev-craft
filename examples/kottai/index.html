Built in 1912, lived in since.

Kottai Veedu was built by a family of bankers who traded across the sea. The hall, the pillars and the tiles are theirs. We restored what the years had taken and added only what a stay needs: quiet rooms, good water and a cook who knows the old recipes.

How the days go.

  • Morning in the kitchen

    7:30 a.m.

    Watch the cook make appam and kuzhi paniyaram on the wood fire, then eat them in the hall.

  • The tile workshop

    11 a.m.

    Visit a family in Athangudi who still pour the tiles by hand, and press one of your own.

  • Afternoon, doing nothing

    2 p.m.

    The courtyard is at its best now. There is a swing, a book and no schedule.

  • Dinner on the verandah

    8 p.m.

    A Chettinad menu of the season, served on banana leaf, lit by lamps.
