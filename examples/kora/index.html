Questions

How does Kora see my transactions?

You can forward bank alerts or import statements. Kora never asks for your banking password.

Where is my data stored?

Encrypted on your device and in an encrypted backup you control. You can export or delete everything at any time.

Can I use it with someone else?

Yes. Family plans share chosen budgets only; everything else stays private to each person.

What happens if I cancel?

You drop to the Free plan and keep your history.
