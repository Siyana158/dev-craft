    Questions parents ask

    Does my child need to be toilet trained?

    No. Our teachers are used to it and will help your child at their own pace.

    What if my child cries at drop-off?

    It is normal. You can stay for the first few mornings and leave a little earlier each day. We will message you once they settle.

    Is food provided?

    We serve a fruit snack. Please send lunch for after school if you need it. We are a nut-free space.

    How do admissions work?

    Visit, meet the teachers, and spend one morning with us. We then offer a place if there is one in the right group. Groups are capped at twelve.

    Come and see a morning.

    Visits are on weekday mornings and take about an hour. Bring your child; they will want to stay.
