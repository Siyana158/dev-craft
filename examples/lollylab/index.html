Questions

Are they made with real fruit?

Yes. Each lolly is whole fruit, a little sugar or jaggery, and water. Nothing artificial.

Do they melt on the way?

The cold box keeps them frozen for 24 hours. We only ship Monday to Wednesday so they never sit over a weekend.

Where do you deliver?

Pondicherry, Chennai and Bengaluru for now. More cities when we have the cold boxes.

Any allergies?

Our kitchen handles nuts and dairy. Each flavour page lists what is in it, and the Tender coconut one contains coconut milk.
